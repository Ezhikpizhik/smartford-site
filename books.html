Never Boring Math

Think Like a Detective, Solve Like an Engineer!

Detectives look for clues. Engineers build solutions. Your child can do both! Every page is a puzzle: crack secret codes, find the fastest route for a busy bee, guide a hungry bee to the honey, decode a secret message, and balance the scales.

  • Counting, adding and taking away
  • Comparing numbers: greater than, less than and equal
  • Patterns, shapes, symmetry and top/front/side views
  • Arrow codes, mazes, decision trees and secret messages
  • Telling time, reading charts and early money math
  • Answer key included
